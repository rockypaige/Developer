Last updated: [Date]

Information We Collect

[Account and session information is used to sign you in and keep your library associated with you. The app may start a guest session; if you choose email sign-in, the authentication service also handles the email and password you provide. When you save research, the app stores the source address, title, extracted article content and available publication details. It also stores folder names and notes, along with basic library details such as item counts and when a folder was edited. When you import a PDF, the app reads it to extract text and saves the resulting document in your library. The app stores the extracted document, not the original PDF as a file.]

How We Use Information

[Your information is used to capture, organize, reopen and export your research, sync your library to your account, and operate and protect the service. This app does not currently include in-app advertising or analytics features.]

Third-Party Services

[When you capture a web address, the app requests that page from its website so it can extract readable content. That website may receive ordinary connection information, such as your network address, and handles the request under its own privacy practices. Your saved library is held by the cloud service used to run this app and is access-controlled for the account that owns it. The app does not make your library public. If you export a document or share it yourself, the recipient can keep their own copy.]

Data Retention and Deletion

[You can remove saved research using the available delete controls in the app. To ask for account deletion or help removing account information, contact the app publisher using the support details provided where you got this app. The publisher can explain how deletion requests and service backups are handled. The service uses account-based access controls to keep one person’s library separate from another’s. No online service can promise absolute security. Saved items remain in your library until they are removed; account and service records may be kept as needed to operate the service, meet legal obligations, or resolve security issues.]

Children's Privacy

[This app is not designed for children under 13, and the publisher does not knowingly ask children under 13 to provide personal information. If you believe a child has provided information, contact the app publisher.]
